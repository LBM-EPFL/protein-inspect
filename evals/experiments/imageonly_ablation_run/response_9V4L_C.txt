The `protein-inspect` heuristic labels its context as "catalytic_likely," but with only a 70-residue helical hairpin and no substrate, no extended pocket visible in the views, and no narrative, **enzymatic function cannot be assigned** from these materials. The two flagged "aspartate dyad" patterns are almost certainly artifacts of the same Zn coordination geometry: the Asp11/Asp45 pair (closest O–O = 3.06 Å) are the two carboxylate Zn ligands, not an independent acid/base dyad, and the Glu8/Glu20 pair (4.39 Å) is geometric-only and unsupported by any catalytic-fold context. I would treat both `aspartate_dyad_geometry` flags as **coincidental geometric matches secondary to the Zn site**, not evidence of an aspartyl protease, glycosidase, or phosphatase mechanism. Mechanism class: **unknown from the available materials**; the Zn site could equally well be structural.
